## The question

How much more does a retailer need to sell after a discount to earn the same gross profit as selling at full price?

## The method

Let **m** be the original gross margin as a share of the full price, and **d** be the discount as a share of that price. When the discount is below the margin, the required volume uplift is:

**required uplift = d / (m − d)**

## The finding

With a 40% gross margin and a 20% discount:

**required uplift = 20% / (40% − 20%) = 100%**

The retailer needs to sell twice the original volume to earn the same gross profit under these assumptions. Try different margins, discounts, and return rates in the [break-even calculator]({{ '/tools/breakeven/' | relative_url }}).

## Caveats

This covers gross profit only. It ignores returns, brand effects, and stock clearance, as well as other costs such as fulfilment and payment fees. A required uplift is a break-even threshold, not a prediction that customers will buy more.
