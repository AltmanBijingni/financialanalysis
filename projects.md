## Promotion break-even calculator

**Summary:** Estimates how much extra order volume a promotion needs to match the gross profit from full-price sales, with an adjustment for different return rates.

**What it shows:** How gross margin, discount depth, and return rates affect the gross-profit break-even hurdle.
